Hot Dog Recipe

The perfect quick meal for any occasion! Including on the go!

Ingredients:

  • Hot dog buns
  • Hot dogs
  • Sliced Cheese
  • Onions
  • Mustard
  • Ketchup
  • Mayo

Cooking Steps:

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil.
  2. Submerge the hot dogs in the boiling water and cook for 3-5 minutes.
  3. Remove the hot dogs from the water and place them in the hot dog buns.
  4. Top with your desired condiments.
