Pizza Recipe

A delicious homemade pizza recipe that is easy to make and perfect for any occasion!

Ingredients:

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese

Optional toppings:

  • Sliced mushrooms
  • Sliced bell peppers
  • Sliced olives
  • Sliced onions
  • Pepperoni slices

Cooking Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Transfer the dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread the tomato sauce over the dough, leaving a border.
  5. Top with shredded mozzarella cheese.
  6. Add any desired optional toppings.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
