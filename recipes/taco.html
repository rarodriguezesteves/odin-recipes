Taco Recipe

A delicious and easy to make taco recipe that is perfect for any occasion!

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup diced onions
  • 1 cup chopped lettuce

Cooking Steps:

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  3. Drain the excess fat from the skillet.
  4. Add the taco seasoning and mix well.
  5. Stir in the diced tomatoes and onions.
  6. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  7. Warm the corn tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  8. Fill each tortilla with the beef mixture and top with shredded cheese and chopped lettuce.
